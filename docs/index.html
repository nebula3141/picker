Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for the shoot sizes that make other viewers choke.

Instant open

Double-click any image in Explorer and it's on screen instantly — no splash, no catalog import. The filmstrip loads in the background.

Every RAW format

Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fuji RAF, DNG, Olympus ORF, Panasonic RW2, Pentax PEF, Samsung SRW — plus all common formats.

Runs on anything

Smart decoding keeps small images full-res and never softer than your screen, while a 60MP RAW that bogs down Lightroom still opens instantly on 8 GB of RAM.

Sort & cull fast

Send picks to destination folders, move, copy, or recycle — with one-key undo and a full right-click menu everywhere. Tear through a 3,000-image shoot.

Pro viewing tools

RGB histogram with clipping warning, focus peaking, 1:1 zoom, crop, rotate, side-by-side compare, and a full EXIF panel — plus open in Photoshop or Lightroom in one click.

Video inline

MP4, MOV and MKV sit right alongside your stills — frame-step, scrub, loop, and 0.25×–4× speed in the same filmstrip and workflow.
