Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for the shoot sizes that make other viewers choke.

Instant open

Double-click any image in Explorer and it's on screen instantly — no splash, no catalog import. The filmstrip loads in the background.

Every RAW format

Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fuji RAF, DNG, Olympus ORF, Panasonic RW2, Pentax PEF, Samsung SRW — plus all common formats.

Runs on anything

Smart decoding keeps small images full-res and never softer than your screen, while a 60MP RAW that bogs down Lightroom still opens instantly on 8 GB of RAM.

Sort & cull fast

Save your sorting folders in Selection, then one key files each photo — copy or move, instantly, with one-tap undo. Plus Ctrl+F search across the whole library. Tear through a 3,000-image shoot.

Pro viewing tools

RGB histogram with clipping warning, focus peaking, 1:1 zoom, crop, rotate, side-by-side compare, and a full EXIF panel — plus open in Photoshop or Lightroom in one click.

Video inline

MP4, MOV and MKV sit right alongside your stills — frame-step, scrub, loop, and 0.25×–4× speed. As of v4.8.0 video support is bundled out of the box, no ffmpeg install needed.

What's new

v4.10.0 — Modern formats, instant loading & a friendlier start

  • HEIC / HEIF / AVIF — iPhone photos and modern formats open, browse, thumbnail and sort like any other image.
  • Animated GIF / WEBP / APNG now play in the viewer, and Show on Map opens a photo's GPS location in your browser.
  • Instant loading — folders open immediately no matter how deep the tree, counts stream in on your CPU cores, and reopening a folder is near-instant. Scrolling stays smooth while a folder loads.
  • Rapid-fire sorting — send a photo (Ctrl+Space / Ctrl+1…9) and it auto-advances to the next; a progress counter and resume-where-you-left-off keep long culls on track.
  • Fully keyboard-driven — arrow-key folder navigation, Enter to open, Escape to step back a level, plus Copy Image (Ctrl+C) to the clipboard.
  • First-run welcome and clear load errors for corrupt or unsupported files. Full list in the changelog.
