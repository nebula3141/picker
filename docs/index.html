Wedding, events, studio — PICker handles the shoot sizes that make other viewers choke.

Instant Open

Double-click any image in Explorer — it's on screen instantly. No splash, no catalog import. The filmstrip loads in the background.

Every RAW Format

Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fuji RAF, DNG, Olympus ORF, Panasonic RW2, Pentax PEF, Samsung SRW. 60MP files on 8GB RAM.

Runs on Anything

Smart 50% decode by default — small images stay full-res and nothing is ever softer than your screen, yet a 60MP RAW that makes Lightroom swap to disk still shows instantly here. Bump to 100% only when pixel-peeping.

Keyboard Culling

1/2/3 sorts to destinations, Enter sends, Ctrl+Z undoes, arrows navigate. Cull a 3,000-image wedding without touching the mouse.

Photographer's Tools

RGB histogram with clipping warning, focus peaking overlay, 1:1 zoom, crop, rotation, side-by-side compare, EXIF info panel.

Video Inline

MP4/MOV/MKV alongside photos. Frame step, speed control (0.25x–4x), loop, scrub. Same filmstrip, same cull workflow.
